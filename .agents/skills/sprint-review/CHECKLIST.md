# Sprint Review — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The sprint goal is restated and its outcome (fully met / partially met / not met) is recorded with a brief factual narrative.
- [ ] Every story from the Sprint Backlog appears in either "Completed work" or "Incomplete work" — none are omitted.
- [ ] Each completed story notes whether all acceptance criteria were met.
- [ ] Each incomplete story records a reason for non-completion and a disposition (return to backlog / carry forward / deprecate).
- [ ] Stakeholder feedback is captured and grouped by theme; at least one piece of feedback is recorded, or the absence of feedback is explicitly noted.
- [ ] Each feedback item is attributed to a named stakeholder or role.
- [ ] Any scope or priority decisions made during the meeting are recorded with the approving party, or the absence of decisions is explicitly noted.
- [ ] At least two next-sprint hints are recorded.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical Scrum Guide reference URL (`https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/sprint-review/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
