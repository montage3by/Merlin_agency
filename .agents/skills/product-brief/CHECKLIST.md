# Product Brief — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The problem statement is concrete and observable — it describes a real, specific pain rather than an abstract category.
- [ ] At least one primary user persona is described with enough specificity to distinguish them from a generic user.
- [ ] At least two success metrics are provided; each is measurable and has a stated target or threshold.
- [ ] At least two risks or critical assumptions are documented, with each marked as verified or hypothesized.
- [ ] The high-level approach section describes the core solution concept clearly enough that a stakeholder could explain it without reading the full document.
- [ ] No template placeholder text remains in any section.
- [ ] Open questions are listed as questions — not resolved prematurely — and each has an assigned owner.
- [ ] The brief is 1–3 pages in length; it does not contain requirements-level detail that belongs in a PRD.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical business-case reference URL (`https://leanstack.com/lean-canvas`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/product-brief/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
