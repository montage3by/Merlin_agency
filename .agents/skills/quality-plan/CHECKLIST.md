# Quality Plan — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] Quality objectives are defined for all three dimensions: user-visible output quality, internal process quality, and documentation quality.
- [ ] Each quality objective is verifiable — there is a clear, unambiguous way to determine whether it has been met.
- [ ] Every quality metric has a numeric or binary target, a measurement method, and a named owner.
- [ ] At least three quality assurance activities (prevention-oriented) are listed with frequency and responsible role.
- [ ] At least two quality control activities (detection-oriented) are listed with a trigger condition and responsible role.
- [ ] The assurance and control sections are distinct — prevention activities are not conflated with detection activities.
- [ ] Any applicable external standards (ISO, accessibility, regulatory) are listed with the deliverables they govern.
- [ ] The artifact cites the canonical quality-management reference URL (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_management`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/quality-plan/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
