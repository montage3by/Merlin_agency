# Product Requirements Document — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The Overview clearly identifies the problem being solved and the proposed solution in two to three paragraphs, drawn from the product brief and charter.
- [ ] Every Goal is verifiable (a test exists that could confirm it was achieved), not merely aspirational.
- [ ] At least two Non-goals are stated, and they align with any out-of-scope items listed later in the document.
- [ ] Each functional requirement (FR-xx) is testable: given a defined condition, the system produces a defined, observable output.
- [ ] Non-functional requirements cover at minimum performance, security, accessibility, and observability, each with a quantified or qualifiable target.
- [ ] Constraints and Assumptions are recorded in separate lists; no assumption appears as a constraint and vice versa.
- [ ] Success metrics are measurable, each owned by a named role, and each has a stated measurement method.
- [ ] The Open Questions log captures any unresolved issues that would block baselining, with an owner and due date for each.
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records the canonical PRD reference URL, the source mode used (offline or online), and contains no fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/prd/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
