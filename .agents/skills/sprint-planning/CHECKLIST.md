# Sprint Planning — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] A sprint goal is present, stated in one sentence, and expresses an outcome rather than a task list.
- [ ] Team capacity is recorded with available person-days per member and notes on any known absences or reduced-capacity factors.
- [ ] The Sprint Backlog table includes story ID, title, estimate, owner, acceptance criteria reference, and dependencies for every committed item.
- [ ] The total committed estimate does not exceed the team's stated effective capacity.
- [ ] At least one risk or dependency is documented, or the absence of risks is explicitly noted.
- [ ] The Definition of Done is recorded and confirmed as achievable for all Sprint Backlog items within the sprint timeframe.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical Scrum Guide reference URL (`https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/sprint-planning/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
